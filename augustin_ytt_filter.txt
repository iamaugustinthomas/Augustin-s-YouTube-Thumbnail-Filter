! Title: Augustine YouTube Thumbnail Filter
! Description: A "filterless filter" that completely removes YouTube thumbnails and their spacing for a pure, text-only feed.
! Version: 1.0
! Expires: 7 days

! Hide main video thumbnails globally (home, search, sidebar)
youtube.com##ytd-thumbnail

! Hide Shorts thumbnails
youtube.com##ytd-reel-item-renderer ytd-thumbnail

! Remove the large skeleton loading placeholders that mimic thumbnails
youtube.com###ghost-cards

! Tighten the gap between videos so it looks like a clean text list
youtube.com##ytd-rich-item-renderer:style(margin-bottom: 12px !important;)
