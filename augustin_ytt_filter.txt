! Title: Augustine YouTube Thumbnail Filter
! Description: A "filterless filter" that completely removes YouTube thumbnails and collapses the grid into a pure text list.
! Version: 1.2
! Expires: 7 days

! 1. Block any image served from ytimg.com (covers thumbnails, previews, Shorts)
||i.ytimg.com^$image

! 2. Collapse empty containers so text flows without gaps
youtube.com##ytd-thumbnail
youtube.com##ytd-playlist-thumbnail
youtube.com##a#thumbnail
youtube.com##yt-image
youtube.com##ytd-rich-grid-media #thumbnail
youtube.com##ytd-video-renderer #thumbnail
youtube.com##ytd-compact-video-renderer #thumbnail
margin: 0 !important; padding-left: 0 !important;)
